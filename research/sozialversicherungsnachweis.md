## Key findings

### 1. Definition & distinction
- **Sozialversicherungsnachweis** = historically meant the Sozialversicherungsausweis (insurance card/document)
- Since 2023-01-01: replaced by **Versicherungsnummernachweis** (insurance number proof), issued by Deutsche Rentenversicherung (German pension insurance, DRV)
- In **posting/cross-border context**: the term actually refers to the **A1 certificate (A1-Bescheinigung)** or residence permit proof for foreign workers
- The Versicherungsnummernachweis remains valid but is purely domestic documentation

### 2. Carrying requirements (per AOK, DRV)
- Old rule (pre-2023): mandatory to carry in certain industries (construction, catering, logistics, etc.)
- **Current rule (post-2023):** NO mandatory physical carrying requirement — employers retrieve the number electronically from DSRV
- Exception: if electronic lookup fails, worker must produce the Versicherungsnummernachweis
- For **foreign workers temporarily posted to Germany:** must carry **residence permit or A1-Bescheinigung** instead, NOT the German insurance number document

### 3. For employers requesting it
- Legal basis: § 4, § 5 SGB IV (Social Code Book IV; Ausstrahlung/Einstrahlung = posting rules)
- Employer obligation since 2023: retrieve number electronically from the Data Centre of the German Pension Insurance (Datenstelle der Rentenversicherung, DSRV)
- Only exception: if DSRV returns no unique match → worker must present Versicherungsnummernachweis or employer requests new number assignment
- Search volume "sozialversicherungsnachweis arbeitgeber" (170/mo, rank 18) reflects this employer compliance need

### 4. Cross-border posting (PWD context — premote angle)
- For employees posted **abroad from Germany:** use A1-Bescheinigung under § 4 SGB IV + EU Regulation 883/2004
- For foreign workers posted **to Germany:** under § 5 SGB IV + EU Regulation 883/2004, they present A1 from home country or residence permit
- The term "Sozialversicherungsnachweis" in posting contexts = A1-Bescheinigung (documented in AOK FAQ and Perplexity research)
