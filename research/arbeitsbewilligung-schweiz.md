## Facts + sources (all opened and read first-hand, 2026-08-24)

### 1. Notification procedure / 8-day rule for posted workers (EU/EFTA)
Source: https://www.sem.admin.ch/sem/de/home/themen/fza_schweiz-eu-efta/meldeverfahren.html (SEM, State Secretariat for Migration (Staatssekretariat für Migration))
- Quote (translated from German): "The activity of posted workers and self-employed service providers is subject to notification if it lasts more than eight days in total within a calendar year."
- Exception (notification obligation from day 1, regardless of duration): main and ancillary construction trades, gardening and landscaping, hospitality (incl. hotel industry), cleaning services in businesses/households, surveillance and security services, itinerant trades (except trade fairs/circuses), erotic industry.
- Notification deadline: at least 8 days before the start of work in Switzerland (emergency exceptions strictly defined: repairs/accidents/natural disasters, deployment within 3 days of the damage occurring).
- The 8 notification-free days as well as the max. 90 working days apply both per company and per posted worker (quota logic with examples in the text).

### 2. Residence and work permit (Aufenthalts- und Arbeitsbewilligung) EU/EFTA (free movement of persons) — threshold 90 days / 3 months
Source: https://www.sem.admin.ch/sem/de/home/themen/fza_schweiz-eu-efta/eu-efta_buerger_schweiz/faq.html (SEM FAQ)
- Quote (translated from German): "EU/EFTA nationals only need a residence permit, which is at the same time the work permit."
- Gainful employment up to 3 months/calendar year: no permit under immigration law required, but notification obligation.
- Gainful employment from 3 months: registration with the municipality of residence within 14 days of arrival and before taking up the job; permit L (contract up to 364 days) or permit B (contract duration ≥1 year or open-ended), depending on the duration of employment.

### 3. Third-country nationals (non-EU/EFTA) — qualification criterion
Source: https://www.sem.admin.ch/sem/de/home/themen/arbeit/nicht-eu_efta-angehoerige.html (SEM)
- Quote (translated from German): "If you come from a non-EU/EFTA state and wish to take up gainful employment in Switzerland, you can only be admitted if you are well qualified. Managers, specialists and other qualified workers are considered well qualified – primarily persons with a university or university of applied sciences degree and several years of professional experience."
- The employer must prove that no suitable persons are available on the domestic labour market or the EU/EFTA labour market (priority for domestic workers, Inländervorrang); pay/social security/working conditions must be in line with local, occupational and industry norms.
- Quotas (annual maximum numbers) exist for third-country permits; exact figures for 2026 not verifiable via direct fetch (PDF source not text-extractable) — therefore NOT cited with a concrete figure in the article, only described as "subject to annual quotas".

### 4. Cantonal competence (example Basel-Stadt)
Source: https://www.bs.ch/themen/arbeit-und-steuern/arbeitsbewilligungen/erstmaliger-stellenantritt/arbeitnehmende-sind-angehoerige-eines-euefta-staates (Canton of Basel-Stadt, content updated 09.07.2026)
- Confirms: cantonal authorities are responsible for permit procedures; separate sections for "employment up to 90 days" vs. "employment over 90 days" (section titles translated from German) — congruent with the SEM federal rules, shows practical cantonal implementation.
- All 26 cantons have their own migration/labour market authorities (migration office, office for economy/labour) — the procedure runs via the canton, not the federal level directly.
