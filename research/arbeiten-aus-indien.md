## Key facts (all sources retrieved and read first-hand)

### 1. German-Indian social security agreement (Sozialversicherungsabkommen, SVA-Indien)
Source: Deutsche Rentenversicherung (German pension insurance, DRV) rvRecht legal literature system — treaty text and overview
https://rvrecht.deutsche-rentenversicherung.de/SharedDocs/rvRecht/05_Normen_und_Vertraege/03_Vertragsrecht/02_Laender/Indien/indien_sva_2011/indien_sva_2011.html
Retrieved: 2026-09-01

Quotes (translated from the German original): "Agreement between the Federal Republic of Germany and the Republic of India on Social Security of 12 October 2011 (BGBl. II 2011 p. 588), entered into force on 1 May 2017 (BGBl. II 2018 p. 484)". The agreement replaces an earlier agreement of 8 October 2008 ("desiring to replace the Agreement of 8 October 2008 ... with a new, more far-reaching agreement").

### 2. Posting period: 48 calendar months (+12 months extension possible)
Source: DRV rvRecht, Article 7 SVA-Indien ("Anzuwendende Rechtsvorschriften bei Entsendung" — applicable legislation in the case of posting)
https://rvrecht.deutsche-rentenversicherung.de/SharedDocs/rvRecht/05_Normen_und_Vertraege/03_Vertragsrecht/02_Laender/Indien/indien_sva_2011/0007/0007_2017_05_01.html
Retrieved: 2026-09-01

Quote para. 1 (translated from German): "...during the first 48 calendar months, solely the legislation of the first Contracting State shall continue to apply as if he were still employed in its territory."
Quote para. 2 (translated from German): "If the duration of the posting exceeds the period of 48 calendar months by no more than twelve further calendar months, the competent authority of the Contracting State to which the employee was posted may, ... upon joint application ..., exempt this employee from the application of the legislation of that Contracting State for the extension period."
Para. 3 lists grounds for exclusion (among others: no posting where the activity does not belong to the employer's line of business, no substantial business activity in the sending state, or where the employee has been employed in the sending state for less than 6 months since the last posting period).

### 3. The agreement covers pension insurance only; posting certificate (Entsendebescheinigung) DE/IN 101
Source: Deutsche Rentenversicherung, country page India ("Arbeiten in Deutschland und Indien" — working in Germany and India)
https://www.deutsche-rentenversicherung.de/DRV/DE/Rente/Ausland/Ansprechpartner-und-Verbindungsstellen/Indien/Indien.html
Retrieved: 2026-09-01

Quote (translated from German): "The German-Indian social security agreement contains provisions on pension insurance only. All other branches of social security are not covered."
Quote on the posting certificate (translated from German): "This so-called posting is, however, limited to 48 months. For this you need, as an employee, self-employed person or civil servant, a posting certificate DE/IN 101. Your employer applies for the posting certificate at the statutory health insurance fund to which your pension insurance contributions are paid. If you are not subject to compulsory pension insurance, Deutsche Rentenversicherung Bund issues the posting certificate."
On the exception agreement (Ausnahmevereinbarung) (translated from German): "If it is clear from the outset that the employment will last longer than 48 months ..., you and your employer can apply for a so-called exception agreement ... For this, contact the Deutsche Verbindungsstelle Krankenversicherung – Ausland (German Liaison Office for Health Insurance – International, DVKA)."
The application must be filed before the activity begins.

### 4. India's Social Security Agreements (SSA) and Certificate of Coverage (COC) via EPFO
Source: Press Information Bureau (PIB), Ministry of Labour & Employment, Government of India — "Covering of Indians Working Abroad Under EPFO"
https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1513306
Retrieved: 2026-09-01

Quote: "This facility is available to Indian workers who are deputed by their employer to the countries with whom India has entered into a Social Security Agreement (SSA) and they take Certificate of Coverage (COC) from Employees' Provident Fund Organization (EPFO)." Germany is one of 18 countries with an SSA with India (list includes Germany). Confirms that the agreement works bilaterally in both directions (also for Indian employees posted to Germany).

### 5. Visa for German business travellers/remote workers in India: e-Business Visa, 180-day limit
Source: IHK Rhein-Neckar (Chamber of Commerce and Industry), "Business Visa für Geschäftsreisende nach Indien" (business visas for business travellers to India)
https://www.ihk.de/rhein-neckar/international/maerkte-international/indien/recht-und-steuern-in-indien/business-visa-941638
Retrieved: 2026-09-01

Quote (translated from German): "German citizens can travel to India with an e-visa. Depending on the activity on site, the following e-visas are usually relevant for trips with a professional background: e-Business Visa (for business travellers) ..."
Quote on validity (translated from German): "The e-Business Visa is valid for one year from the date of issue and allows multiple entries. Continuous stay during one visit must not exceed 180 days; otherwise, registration with the relevant foreigners' authority FRO (Foreigners Registration Office) must take place within 14 days after the 180 days have elapsed."
Quote on the Employment Visa (translated from German): "The rules for Employment Visas stipulate that, where the validity exceeds 180 days, registration with the Indian foreigners' authority is required within 14 days of entry. If, however, the work visa is valid for only 180 days or less, no registration is required."
Quote on the purpose of the Business Visa according to the Indian Embassy (translated from German): "Foreigners who wish to travel to India for employment purposes receive a Business Visa if they draw a salary outside India (i.e. from their German company)."

### 6. General entry requirements (context, no key figure)
Source: Federal Foreign Office (Auswärtiges Amt), "Indien: Reise- und Sicherheitshinweise" (India: travel and safety advice)
https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/de/reiseundsicherheit/indiensicherheit-205998
Retrieved: 2026-09-01
Confirms: no visa on arrival for German nationals; digital E-Arrival Card mandatory since 1 October 2025 (Immigration and Foreigners Act, 2025); visa must be held before entry (including as an e-visa).
